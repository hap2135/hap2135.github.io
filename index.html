About

I'm finishing an MS in electrical engineering at Columbia after completing a BA in physics and a BS in electrical engineering as a 3+2 student with Colgate and Columbia University. I've had the opportunity to co-op with Cisco Meraki's IoT and image quality team, work on revolutionary HVAC sensing hardware for Thalo Labs, and restore aging wind energy projects with PivotGen.

I owe a lot to Columbia's electric Formula SAE team, which was my first engineering club when I got to Columbia. I started as the high-voltage charging systems lead and finished by running the high-voltage subsystem during my second year. Managing four subteams taught me that the best design almost never comes from me alone. It came from a culture of disagreeing and collaborating well with others: a methodology that I continue to uphold today.
